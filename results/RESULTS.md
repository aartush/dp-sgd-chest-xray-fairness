## Headline comparisons

Measurement floor: ±3.0 points of binomial standard error on minority recall (n=234). A difference is called a *finding* below only when it is larger than this floor **and** every seed moved in the same direction; otherwise it is reported as *within noise*.

- Vanilla DP-SGD at ε=1: minority recall 0.655 vs 0.705 without DP (delta -0.050, beyond the noise floor).
- Vanilla DP-SGD at ε=3: minority recall 0.685 vs 0.705 without DP (delta -0.020, WITHIN the noise floor).
- Vanilla DP-SGD at ε=8: minority recall 0.715 vs 0.705 without DP (delta +0.010, WITHIN the noise floor).
- `+ class weights` at ε=3: minority recall 0.735 (+0.050 vs vanilla DP-SGD, beyond the noise floor).
- `+ pretrained init` at ε=3: minority recall 0.652 (-0.033 vs vanilla DP-SGD, beyond the noise floor).
- `+ both` at ε=3: minority recall 0.741 (+0.056 vs vanilla DP-SGD, beyond the noise floor).

**The privacy result is not subject to this floor.** MIA AUC is computed over the full train and test splits with a per-class average, its seed-to-seed standard deviation is an order of magnitude smaller than the DP effect, and it moves monotonically with ε. That is the finding this project can defend at 3 seeds; the recall deltas are suggestive and are reported with their uncertainty rather than as established effects.
